Four things it does well

Everything else is a plain, fast list.

Pin and order

Search Google Places or click the map. Each stop keeps a time, a cost and a note; drag to reorder the day.

ปักหมุด จัดลำดับ ใส่เวลาและค่าใช้จ่าย

Ask for what's nearby

"Ramen under ¥1,500 near the station" returns real places with coordinates and a one-line reason. Tick to add.

AI แนะนำร้าน/จุดเที่ยวใกล้แผนวันนั้น

Upload the booking

A flight e-ticket or hotel PDF becomes airports and hotel nights on the right days, plus a line in the budget.

อัปโหลดตั๋ว/ที่พัก ระบบปักหมุดให้อัตโนมัติ

Play the route

Directions draws the real route; a walking or driving figure moves along it so you can feel the day's pacing.
